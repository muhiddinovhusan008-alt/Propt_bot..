Unit 1. Present Simple vs Present Continuous

Present Simple is used for repeated actions, facts, habits, and permanent situations. Formula: Subject + Verb (s/es). Signal words: always, usually, often, every day, sometimes.

Present Continuous is used for actions happening right now at the moment of speaking or temporary situations. Formula: Subject + am/is/are + Verb-ing. Signal words: now, at the moment, look!, listen!

• I play football every Sunday. (Habit)
• Listen! She is singing a song. (Action happening now)

Asosiy tushunchalar:

  • Stative verbs (like, know, understand, believe) are not usually used in Continuous form.
  • Third person singular takes -s/-es: He works, She teaches.

Unit 2. Past Simple vs Present Perfect

Past Simple expresses finished actions at a specific time in the past: I visited Samarkand last year. Signal words: yesterday, ago, in 2020, last week.

Present Perfect links the past with the present; the action happened at an unspecified time, or has a direct result now: I have lost my key (I don't have it now). Formula: have/has + V3 (past participle).

Asosiy tushunchalar:

  • Regular verbs: add -ed (played, visited).
  • Irregular verbs: see, saw, seen | go, went, gone | write, wrote, written.

Unit 3. Conditionals (If Clauses)

Zero Conditional: General truths. If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.

First Conditional: Real future possibilities. If it rains tomorrow, I will stay at home. (If + Present Simple, will + Verb).

Second Conditional: Imaginary or unlikely situations now. If I had a million dollars, I would travel the world. (If + Past Simple, would + Verb).

Asosiy tushunchalar:

  • In First Conditional, do not use 'will' directly inside the 'if' clause.
  • Use 'were' instead of 'was' in Second conditional: 'If I were you...'
